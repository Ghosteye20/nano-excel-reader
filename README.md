# Nano Excel Reader

Chat with your Excel files using an AI that runs entirely on your own computer.
Open a spreadsheet, ask questions in plain English, and get answers that point
to the sheet and row they came from.

## Download

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/Ghosteye20/nano-excel-reader/releases/latest)**
(free, Windows 10/11, 64-bit)

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended)
- About 5 GB free disk space
- Internet once, for the first-run AI model download (about 2.5 GB)
- No graphics card needed. An NVIDIA card is used automatically if present.

## Privacy

Your Excel files and questions never leave your computer. No account, no cloud.
The only internet use is the one-time AI model download.

## Install

1. Download `NanoExcelReader-Setup-1.0.exe` from the link above.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.
   This appears because the app is new and not yet code-signed.
3. No administrator rights are needed.

## Known limitations

- Good at finding and listing information; weaker at counting and totals.
  Check important numbers in Excel.
- Answers can sometimes be wrong. Verify against your original file.
- Without a graphics card, an answer can take a minute or more.
